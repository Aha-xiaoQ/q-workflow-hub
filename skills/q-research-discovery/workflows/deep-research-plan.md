# Deep Research Plan

Use when the topic is broad, ambiguous, high-impact, or likely to need multiple
evidence layers.

## Steps

1. Restate the decision or artifact the research should support.
2. Split the topic into 3-6 subquestions.
3. Choose source roles for each subquestion:
   - primary documentation or official source;
   - implementation example;
   - independent analysis;
   - failure report or counterexample;
   - adjacent-domain source.
4. Define stop conditions before collecting too much material:
   - enough evidence to decide;
   - sources disagree and the disagreement is now explicit;
   - the next step requires user preference or local testing.
5. Search, read, and synthesize by subquestion.
6. Produce a plan with evidence, gaps, and a validation path.

## Output

- Research objective.
- Subquestions.
- Source plan.
- Key findings.
- Disagreements or weak assumptions.
- Recommended next action and validation.
