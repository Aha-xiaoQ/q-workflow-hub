# Flow expression and unseen transfer

Use this workflow only when ordinary creation repeatedly fails on stiffness,
contact, or support; when a high-risk final/mastery/integration claim needs
full-body proof; or when an unseen original transfer is explicitly required.
Delegate source/license posture and release status to `learn-create-review.md`.
For a cast, apply this workflow to the hardest action first, then run
`character-cast-gates.md` for the ensemble.

## Choose a mode

### Study / mastery / unseen-transfer

Run F1-F6. Use controlled positive/negative examples to prove the mechanism,
then transfer it to a new body, age, action, costume, prop, palette, and light.

### Production rescue / high-risk final

Run only the earliest failed stage through F5, then replay the named semantic
locks on the final candidate. Do not require a controlled negative or F6 unseen
transfer unless the user claims mastery/general transfer or explicitly asks for
it.

## F1 - Intent and support

1. Observe approved work at normal scale, then hide it.
2. Draw no more than the head direction, spine, shoulder/pelvis axes, support
   limb, free limb, ground, center-of-mass projection, and minimum functional
   prop direction/contact.
3. Review the unlabeled image first. Freeze the first reading.
4. Pass only when an independent reviewer recovers the concrete action family,
   dominant direction, and support side. A caption cannot repair a mismatch.
5. Keep a static center-of-mass projection inside the support area. Allow a
   dynamic projection outside only when momentum, reaction force, or the next
   landing/contact visibly explains it.

## F2 - Rhythm without hidden pose changes

Freeze the joint skeleton, action, support, and contacts. Compare stiff and
fluent contour rhythms through unequal straights/curves, offset apexes,
compression, stretch, and negative space. Use a same-skeleton diff or mask as
study evidence; do not treat its number as an aesthetic score.

## F3 - Continuous mass relay

Build head, ribcage, pelvis, limbs, hands, prop, and support as overlapping,
tapered masses. Require an entering direction, exiting direction, and visible
overlap/compression result at each major joint. Reject bearing-circle joints,
rod limbs, complete internal part outlines, false segmented silhouettes, and
floating contacts. Review true merged silhouette and grayscale masses.

## F4 - Broad values

Use a deliberately restricted value study to prove planes, overlap, contact,
compression, and the action path. Make each action own its value topology;
preserving the silhouette with a generic near/body/far template does not pass.
Any exact color count used here is task evidence, not a universal formula.

## F5 - Native pixel phrasing

Refine only borders that carry rhythm, weight, contact, turning, or material.
Preserve semantic corners at tools, grips, feet, knees, compressed elbows, and
occlusions. Avoid accidental parallel bands, mechanical equal-step curves,
unnecessary smoothing shells, and purposeless isolated pixels.

In study/mastery mode, prove the lesson with rendered evidence:

- show the original, minimal positive repair or itemized justified freeze,
  negative control, and changed-pixel view at native size;
- verify that the negative really changes pixels and actually depicts the named
  defect; do not pass labels, declared coordinate lists, or straw-man noise;
- report silhouette/palette/connectivity metrics only as evidence for that
  artifact. Do not ban meaningful one-pixel highlights or legitimate contour
  changes in unrelated work.

## F6 - Unseen original transfer

Use only in study/mastery mode. Change body type, age, action, support,
costume, prop, palette, and lighting. Do not reuse source coordinates,
silhouette, palette, accessories, or identity. Before synthesis, pass the
applicable design evidence:

- no-prop or minimum-prop silhouette for action and weight;
- same-skull face/hair variants when age, identity, or expression is in scope;
- broad value planes for flat versus round forms and contact shadows;
- focal light/color budget when a visual focus is in scope.

Passing this evidence authorizes synthesis, not final approval.

## Final semantic replay

For high-risk final/mastery/integration, name and freeze the semantic locks,
then replay them after material and color rendering under the same native size,
crop, background, and prop condition. Compare color, grayscale, silhouette,
and no-prop/minimum-prop views as applicable. A prop-defined action may retain
the minimum functional prop; use the no-prop view chiefly to test action family,
weight, and false appendages.

Reject the final candidate when color or material turns a knee into a shoe,
cloth into a third arm, a hand into a floating fist, or a focused face into a
different age/expression, even if the earlier evidence board passed.

## Review and stop rules

- Review native 1x blind before titles, zooms, answers, or metrics.
- Use both a strict native/action reviewer and an art-direction reviewer for
  mastery or high-risk final claims.
- Return to the earliest failed structural stage; do not patch detail over it.
- Keep failed candidates as private evidence.
- Require Xiao Q review before personal-avatar, public, or game integration.
